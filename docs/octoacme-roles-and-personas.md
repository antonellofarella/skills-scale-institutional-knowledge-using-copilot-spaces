# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads provide technical direction, align architecture with business goals, and help the team turn product priorities into executable delivery plans.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction and architectural guardrails
- Lead design and technical decision-making for the team
- Coordinate implementation dependencies across squads or services
- Support developers with technical mentorship and escalation paths
- Help balance delivery speed, quality, and maintainability

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound solutions that meet product goals
- Reduce avoidable rework and delivery bottlenecks
- Maintain a healthy engineering operating rhythm

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical planning sessions
- Dependency tracking in planning and standups
- Escalations for design constraints, risk, and delivery trade-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to translate priorities into feasible technical plans.
- Partners with Project Managers to assess scope, sequencing, and risk.
- Guides Developers on implementation quality and technical standards.

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads ensure product changes meet quality expectations by defining test strategy, validation plans, and release readiness standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy for functional and non-functional requirements
- Review acceptance criteria and support test case development
- Coordinate test execution and defect triage across teams
- Validate release readiness and identify quality risks before launch
- Maintain visibility into quality trends and recurring issues

### Goals
- Reduce defects that reach production
- Align quality validation with business outcomes and user expectations
- Improve confidence in each release

### Typical Communication
- QA planning sessions and defect triage meetings
- Release readiness reviews and test status reports
- Acceptance criteria discussions with Product and Engineering stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to confirm that requirements are testable and measurable.
- Collaborates with Developers to diagnose defects and improve quality upstream.
- Shares quality risks and readiness signals with Project Managers and stakeholders.

---

## Security & Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security & Compliance Leads ensure project work meets required security, privacy, and compliance obligations throughout the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security requirements and control expectations early in the project
- Coordinate security reviews, threat assessments, and risk mitigation
- Support compliance obligations, audits, and policy alignment
- Partner with teams on remediation plans and safe deployment practices
- Monitor security risk in delivery decisions and release readiness

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance exposure
- Embed secure-by-default practices in delivery work
- Support safe, trusted delivery to customers and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and risk assessments
- Compliance or policy coordination with leadership and stakeholders
- Escalation paths for vulnerabilities and control gaps

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Engineering Leads and Developers on secure design and implementation choices.
- Works with Project Managers and Product Managers on risk awareness and decision timelines.
- Coordinates with Release Managers on pre-deployment checks and rollback considerations.

---

## UX / Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads shape the user experience and help teams ensure that product decisions are valuable, usable, and consistent.

### Responsibilities
- Define user flows, design principles, and interaction patterns
- Translate customer and stakeholder needs into usable product experiences
- Collaborate on feasibility trade-offs with Product and Engineering teams
- Validate designs against usability and accessibility standards
- Maintain design consistency across features and initiatives

### Goals
- Improve user satisfaction and product adoption
- Ensure experience quality is treated as a core delivery outcome
- Align product decisions with customer needs and usability goals

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and research synthesis sessions
- Product planning and backlog refinement conversations
- User feedback and usability testing debriefs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to translate customer insight into backlog priorities.
- Works with Developers and Engineering Leads to ensure designs are feasible and testable.
- Supports Project Managers by clarifying scope, dependencies, and user impact.

---

## Release Managers / Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release Managers / Operations Leads coordinate deployment readiness, release execution, and operational continuity during and after production changes.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release windows and deployment sequencing
- Define rollback and contingency strategies for production changes
- Coordinate release communications, readiness checks, and post-deploy validation
- Track operational risk and support incident readiness
- Align deployment practices with team capacity and stakeholder expectations

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and production surprises
- Keep releases predictable and highly observable
- Improve operational readiness across the delivery lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and deployment checklists
- Post-deploy verification and incident communication updates
- Status reporting to Project Managers and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Engineering Leads and Developers to confirm release readiness and execution plans.
- Partners with Project Managers to align release timing with broader milestones and stakeholders.
- Coordinates with Security and Compliance Leads to ensure safe release conditions.
